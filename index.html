The C10 story

MCT oil is a category. Tricaprin is specific.

C8, C10, C12, and mixed MCT oils are often discussed as if they are interchangeable. They are not. Tricaprin is a triglyceride form of the C10 fatty acid decanoic acid, also called capric acid.

Research, not hype

The heart-health interest comes from a specific research trail.

The strongest published tricaprin research is not in the general wellness population. It centers on specific cardiovascular-metabolism conditions, especially triglyceride deposit cardiomyovasculopathy, or TGCV. That distinction matters.

European Heart Journal 2023

Case report: coronary atherosclerosis regression after tricaprin intake in TGCV

Researchers reported two TGCV cases where dietary tricaprin followed diagnosis, with symptom improvement and regression of diffuse coronary atherosclerosis described in the report.

What the science does — and does not — show

Strong enough to be interesting. Specific enough to be honest.

What it supports

Tricaprin has a serious research story in cardiovascular metabolism, lipid handling, and TGCV-focused studies.

What it does not prove

These studies do not prove that tricaprin treats, prevents, cures, or reverses heart disease in the general population.

That is why this site focuses on research education and cardiovascular wellness interest — not disease-treatment claims.
