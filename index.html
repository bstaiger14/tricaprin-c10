The studies behind the attention.

The strongest published tricaprin research is in specific cardiovascular conditions involving triglyceride metabolism. We summarize the findings carefully, with limits clearly stated.

2023 European Heart Journal

Two TGCV cases brought tricaprin into the spotlight.

A case report described patients with TGCV who began dietary tricaprin after diagnosis, with reported symptom improvement and regression of diffuse coronary atherosclerosis.

Important limit: This was not a large general-population supplement trial.

2025 Nature Cardiovascular Research

Long-term TGCV registry findings expanded the story.

Researchers reported long-term survival and durable heart-failure recovery in TGCV patients treated with supplemental tricaprin in registry studies.

Important limit: TGCV is a specific disease context; broader wellness use requires more study.
