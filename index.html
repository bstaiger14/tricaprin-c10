The research that created attention

Tricaprin’s strongest story is heart-metabolism research — not generic supplement hype.

The most interesting published work is in triglyceride deposit cardiomyovasculopathy (TGCV), a rare cardiovascular condition involving abnormal intracellular triglyceride handling. That does not prove tricaprin treats heart disease in the general population, but it does explain why C10 tricaprin is getting attention.

European Heart Journal 2023

Case report: coronary lesion regression in TGCV

Two patients diagnosed with TGCV started dietary tricaprin. The authors reported symptom improvement within months and imaging findings consistent with regression of atherosclerotic lesions and reduced lipid involvement.

Important: This was a small case report in a specific diagnosed condition, not a general consumer heart-health trial.
View source
Nature Cardiovascular Research 2025

Long-term survival and heart-failure recovery in TGCV

Osaka University described a registry-based study comparing diagnosed TGCV patients treated with tricaprin against controls, reporting higher survival and durable recovery markers in this rare disease context.

Important: This is powerful context for why researchers care about tricaprin, but it is not proof of broad disease treatment for everyday supplement users.
View PubMed
Clinical protocol 2024

First-in-human AAA trial design with tricaprin

A study protocol proposed evaluating daily oral tricaprin in patients with small abdominal aortic aneurysms. This is useful because it shows continued research interest beyond a single paper.

Important: A protocol is not an outcome study. It describes planned research; it does not prove efficacy.
